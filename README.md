## Start with the active, not the packaging

Neutrogena's face washes break down by what's actually in them, and there are only a few buckets that matter.

**Salicylic acid (BHA) cleansers** treat acne. The 2% versions are the real treatment products; they're oil-free and designed for oily, breakout-prone skin. Neutrogena also sells a lower-strength 0.5% version in some markets, which is a gentler starting point but not the same product.

**Hyaluronic acid cleansers** (the Hydro Boost line) are built around hydration. Fragrance-free versions are aimed at normal, sensitive, and dry skin. They won't fight acne, and they aren't supposed to.

**CICA and barrier-focused cleansers** (Ultra Gentle) sit in the sensitive-skin lane: fragrance-free, soap-free, dye-free, non-comedogenic.

**Plain mild cleansers** — Liquid Neutrogena is the long-running one — are for people who just want their face clean without actives or foam.

If you're treating acne, you want bucket one. If your face stings after washing, you're probably in the wrong bucket. That's the whole decision, and everything below is detail.

### Oil-Free Pink Grapefruit Acne Face Wash, 9.1 fl oz

Salicylic acid combined with glycolic and polyhydroxy acids, fragrance-free. This is the exfoliating option — a stronger daily choice, and not the one to start with if your skin is already irritated.

## Straight answers to the questions that come up

**Is Hydro Boost too moisturizing for oily skin?**
It won't treat oil, but it won't cause it either — it's non-comedogenic and oil-free in formula terms. If oil control is the goal, you want a salicylic acid cleanser, not a hydrating gel.

**Can I use a 2% salicylic acid cleanser twice a day?**
Start once a day, at night, and see how your skin responds over two to three weeks. Cleansers rinse off, so contact time is short, but 2% BHA daily is still an active. If you're also using a leave-on exfoliant, don't stack both on day one.

**Is the fragrance-free Hydro Boost a different formula or just a different label?**
It's a genuine variant, not marketing — the ingredient list is built without fragrance, parabens, sulfates, phthalates, and alcohol. If you're sensitive to fragrance, buy that specific version, not the standard gel.

**Where is Neutrogena face wash cheapest?**
Mass retailers and Amazon trade the lowest price back and forth, and 3-packs usually beat singles per ounce. Warehouse sizes and Instacart listings can look cheaper until you notice the per-ounce math runs the other way — one Instacart listing showed the Ultra Gentle 16 fl oz at $22.99, well above Walmart's $9.97 for 12 fl oz.

**Does an expensive cleanser clean better?**
A cleanser's job is removing oil, dirt, and sunscreen without wrecking your barrier. A $10 Neutrogena bottle does that. What you're paying extra for elsewhere is texture, scent, and actives you'll rinse down the drain anyway.
